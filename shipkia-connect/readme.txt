Connect your WooCommerce store to ShipKia to automate shipping, sync tracking info, and provide real-time updates to your customers.

== Description ==

ShipKia is India’s smart shipping aggregator built for WooCommerce sellers who want to ship faster, cheaper, and more reliably. It covers 27,000+ pin codes across India and helps you manage your entire order fulfillment from one powerful dashboard.

With the free ShipKia WooCommerce plugin, you can easily sync your WooCommerce store and automate all your shipping processes, including order import, address validation, label printing, pickup scheduling, and real-time tracking. No technical setup hassles. Get connected and start shipping in minutes.

Choose from multiple reliable courier partners with intelligent recommendations based on cost, speed, and performance. Reduce failed deliveries and revenue loss with advanced AI-powered NDR management, automated customer confirmations, and smart reattempt scheduling.

### Key Features
* Automatic order sync from your WooCommerce store
* AI-driven NDR & RTO recovery to significantly lower return rates
* Real-time tracking with automated notifications to customers
* Bulk label printing, dimension-based weight optimization, and easy manifest generation
* Fast COD remittance with automated settlements and no hidden charges
* Advanced analytics on shipping costs, RTO trends, and delivery performance
* Smart address validation to minimize errors and disputes
* Automated pickup requests and delivery management

### How to integrate your Woocommerce store with ShipKia?

**USING THE SHIPKIA PANEL (Recommended)**
1. Login to the ShipKia panel.
2. Go to Dashboard → Channels → Add New Store.
3. Select WooCommerce and click “Connect WooCommerce Store”.
4. Enter your Shop URL. The plugin handles the authentication flow securely.
5. Once approved, click “Sync Now” to start importing your historical orders.

**VIA THE PLUGIN SETTINGS**
1. **Activation:** Once activated, go to the settings page (WooCommerce → ShipKia Connect).
2. **Initialization:** Click the "Initialize Store Connection" button. The plugin will check for any existing registration on the ShipKia platform.
3. **Automatic Discovery:** If your store is already connected to ShipKia (based on domain), the plugin will automatically map your account. No manual keys needed!
4. **New Registration:** If no existing connection is found, the plugin prompts you to create one. It will then automatically generate WooCommerce REST API keys and register your store with ShipKia instantly.

**Plugin Connection States & Behavior**
* **Not Connected:** Shows an "Initialize Store Connection" prompt. No data is synced until the connection is established.
* **Connected & Active:** Displays your unique Store ID and connection health (API & Webhooks). Orders and tracking status sync automatically in the background.
* **Auto-Verification:** The plugin periodically checks connection health to ensure your tracking updates are never missed.

**Verify ShipKia Integration via WooCommerce REST API & Webhooks**
1. Go to WooCommerce → Settings → Advanced.
2. Open **REST API** to find the ShipKia integration key. Depending on your connection method, it will be named either:
   - **“ShipKia Integration”** (if connected via ShipKia Panel)
   - **“ShipKia Integration - Autogenerated (Timestamp)”** (if initialized via plugin settings)
3. Open **Webhooks** to verify that topics (Order/Product Created/Updated) are active and pointing to the ShipKia endpoint.

Once connected, ShipKia handles the heavy lifting, syncing orders automatically so you can focus on growing your business.

== External Services ==

This plugin connects to the ShipKia Platform and BU eCommerce Integrations API. These external services are required to connect a WooCommerce store to ShipKia, create or verify the store registration, create WooCommerce webhooks, synchronize plugin settings, update connection status, and disconnect or remove the store connection when requested by an administrator.

The plugin sends data to these services when an administrator initializes or reconnects the store connection, clicks "Sync Now", changes plugin settings, disconnects/removes the store, or completes WooCommerce authorization. WooCommerce also sends webhook payloads to the ShipKia Platform when configured order or product events occur.

Data sent may include the store domain and URL, store identifier, platform name, WooCommerce REST API consumer key and secret, plugin-generated authentication secret/signature, access and refresh tokens used by the integration, plugin settings such as tracking button options, connection status, webhook status, timestamps, and diagnostic connection logs used to troubleshoot setup. WooCommerce webhook payloads may include order and product data needed for shipping, fulfillment, tracking, and synchronization.

These services are provided by ShipKia / BUOPSO. Please review the service terms and privacy policy before connecting your store:

* Terms of Service: https://shipkia.com/terms
* Privacy Policy: https://shipkia.com/privacy

== Installation ==

1. Upload the plugin files to the `/wp-content/plugins/shipkia-connect` directory.
2. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' menu in WordPress.
3. Recommended: Go to the ShipKia Panel to connect your store. Alternatively, go to **WooCommerce > ShipKia Connect** and click **Initialize Store Connection**.
